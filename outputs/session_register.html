Two different questions

Verdicts applied means the good / MUA / noise calls were written into the group column of cluster_info.tsv — never into cluster_group.tsv, and never by hand.

Bpod synced means scripts/sync_session.py wrote a synced/ directory aligning the ephys to the behaviour, and that its nine verdict checks passed. A session can have one without the other, and treating them as one question is how “is it synced?” gets answered wrongly.

A joined session has no DIO and no Bpod file of its own, so it is never synced in its own folder. It is synced once per half, each half against its own behaviour, from the joint sort restricted to that half — one unit space, two trial tables. Those rows are marked [from concat].

What the sync check means

A synced/ folder existing says nothing about whether the two clocks agree. The check reports three things from that dataset’s own session_meta.json: the fraction of Bpod events that found their Trodes TTL (anything under 100 % means events went unexplained), the worst-case leave-one-out error of the time conversion — each matched pair predicted from all the others, which is the honest accuracy figure — and how many trials the pipeline was willing to call synced.

A dataset passes when every event matched, every trial is valid, and that worst-case error is under 50 ms. All of them pass, at 29–48 ms worst case and a median error of essentially zero.

Each session_meta.json also carries a sync_status of needs_review, and that is about a different number: the residual of fitting ONE straight line across the whole session, which is hundreds of milliseconds because the two clocks drift apart. That is exactly why the conversion is local interpolation rather than a single line, and why the holdout error above is the figure to read.

Keeping this current

Verdicts live in each review page’s own database on claude.ai. Nothing on disk knows about them until they are dumped, so this register compares the two and says drifted when the page is ahead of Z.

Say the word after a reviewing session and the three collections get dumped to <session>.kilosort/review/verdicts/ on Z, hashed on both sides, and this page is regenerated. The markdown copy at post_ks_correction/SESSION_STATUS.md gives git the history.

Nothing on this page is typed by hand: every figure comes from outputs/session_status.json, which is read off Z.
